3. What is hoisting in Javascript?
    Hoisting is JavaScript's default behavior of moving declarations
    (variables and functions) to the top of their containing scope
    The Key Thing to Remember: Only the declarations are hoisted, 
    not the initializations (assignments of values).

    1. Variable Hoisting
    When a var variable is hoisted, it is initialized with the value undefined.

    2. let and const Hoisting (The "Temporal Dead Zone")
    let and const are also hoisted, but they are not initialized.

    3.Function Hoisting
    Function declarations are fully hoisted—their entire definition is moved to the top. 
    This means you can call a function before it's declared in your code.
